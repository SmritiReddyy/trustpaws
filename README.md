# TrustPaws 🐾

> **Grooming transparency for pet parents. Real-time visibility into every step of your pet's spa visit.**

**Live at → [smritireddyy.github.io/trustpaws](https://smritireddyy.github.io/trustpaws)**

---

## What is TrustPaws?

TrustPaws is a pet spa transparency platform built for [Heads Up For Tails](https://www.headsupfortails.com/). It bridges the gap between grooming staff and pet parents — giving parents live, step-by-step updates on their pet's appointment without having to call the front desk.

Staff log every stage of the grooming process. Parents track it in real time.

---

## Features

### For Pet Parents
- **Live status tracking** — see exactly what stage your pet is at: Checked In → Bathing → Grooming → Drying → Ready for Pickup
- **Service-by-service updates** — each service is checked off as it's completed, with groomer notes visible to you
- **Groomer observations** — staff log condition notes (coat quality, sensitive areas, behaviour) after every service
- **Full incident transparency** — any incident, no matter how minor, is logged and shown to you with the action taken
- **Visit history** — all past visits in one place: date, services received, groomer, session duration, and notes
- **Shareable tracking link** — each appointment has a unique link you can open anytime, no login needed
- **Parent portal** — log in once to see all your pets, active appointments, and visit history in one dashboard

### For Grooming Staff
- **Daily dashboard** — see today's appointments at a glance with live status
- **Service completion forms** — after each service, log how it went (Good / Sensitive area / Needs attention) plus free-text observations
- **Step-by-step status updates** — move appointments through the grooming pipeline with one tap
- **Incident logging** — log incidents with severity levels (Low / Medium / High) and actions taken
- **Pet & parent management** — full profiles for every pet and their owner
- **Shareable tracking links** — copy and send a parent their live tracking link instantly
- **Live monitor** — real-time overview of all active appointments
